Features

Built to learn and test circuits

For students, teachers and hobbyists who want to see what a circuit does, not only read numbers.

Live burn-out effects

LEDs, bulbs, fans, buzzers, capacitors, inductors and fuses fail when pushed past their limits.

Circuit and NGSpice code

Draw a schematic to get a netlist, or write NGSpice code to generate the circuit.

Electron flow

Animated current shows the direction and speed of charge through the circuit.

Measurement tools

Digital multimeter, ammeter and voltmeter for voltage, current, resistance, inductance and capacitance.

Relays, transformers, buzzers

Real-life examples including step-down supplies, isolation, rectifiers and relay drivers.

Save and export

Open and save .cir netlists. Export the circuit and graphs as SVG or PNG. Light and dark themes included.

How it works

FAQ

Common questions

Is the simulator free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT License.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the simulator in a modern browser and start.

Why does it ask me to sign in?

Google sign-in is needed to edit component properties, place parts, create new circuits, save or export, and view or edit NGSpice code.

What happens when a part burns out?

It smokes, burns and stays an open circuit. Press Undo to restore it, then fix the cause, for example a missing resistor or the wrong supply voltage.

Can I use my own NGSpice netlist?

Yes. Open a .cir file and the circuit is generated from it.
