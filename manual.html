User Manual

Getting Started

Open the simulator in any modern browser and follow these steps. No installation is needed.

Open the simulator

Click Simulator in the menu. Sign in with Google to edit component properties, place components, create circuits, save or export, and view or edit NGSpice code.

Choose a ready example

Use Select Topic, then Select Example, at the top to load one of the included circuits.

Build or edit a circuit

Place components from the toolbar, connect them with wires, then select a part to change its value and burn limits in the Properties panel.

Run and observe

Watch electron flow, meter readings, graphs and live effects such as a glowing bulb, a spinning fan or a burning LED. Use the Sound button to switch buzzer and fan sound on or off.

Save your work

Save the netlist as a .cir file, reopen it later, or export the circuit and simulation graph as SVG or PNG.

Tips

  • If a part burns out, press Undo to restore it, then raise its limit or reduce the source.
  • Use the theme button in the top bar to switch between light and dark mode.
  • For measuring inductance and capacitance, see the Docs.
